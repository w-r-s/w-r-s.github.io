I am a first-year Ph.D. student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, supervised by Prof. Benyou Wang. My research focuses on exploring trustworthy medical large language models (LLMs) and multimodal large models (MLLMs), as well as exploring multimodal generative for healthcare applications. I am a perfectionist. I want to solve the problems in my hands in perfect ways, which is, however, not achievable in many situations.
